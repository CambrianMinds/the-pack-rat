GAME DOCUMENTATION & CONTROLS

STORY & OBJECTIVE

You are The Pack Rat, a debonair little rodent with a predilection for shiny little things! Your goal is to collect as many baubles as you can, navigate puzzles and hazards, and find the Mouse Hole to advance to next levels.

BAUBLES & POINTS

  • Buttons: 10 points
  • Silver Coins: 25 points
  • Gold Coins: 50 points
  • Cookies: 100 points
  • Cheeses: 250 points
  • Level Completion: 500 bonus points!
  • Extra Life: Awarded every 10,000 points!

CONTROLS

  • ← → or A D : Move Left and Right
  • Space, Ctrl, or Z : Jump
  • Alt, Shift, or X : Push objects and movable crates
  • ↑ or W : Look Up (scroll camera view up)
  • ↓ or S : Look Down & Dislodge fragile blocks beneath you
  • M or Z : Full Level Zoom / Minimap mode
  • K : Kill Pack Rat (if trapped)
  • F1 : Help Menu | F2 : Sound On/Off | F3 or Esc : Pause
  • F10 : Toggle Score HUD

TIPS & SECRETS

• Pipes & Tubes: Jump into pipe entrances to ride high-speed tube transport networks!

• Illusions: Not everything is what it seems. Some walls and water pools are illusionary passages leading to secret rooms!

• Mousetraps & Hazards: Watch out for sharp mousetraps, live electrical currents, deep water, and patrolling enemy rats.
