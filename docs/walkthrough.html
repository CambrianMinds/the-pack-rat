Campaign & Secret Network

Unlike traditional linear games, The Pack Rat branches extensively across the house. There are four distinct pathways through the world:

🏛 The Core Spine (Levels 1 – 4)

Every playthrough begins in Level 1 and advances linearly through Level 4. You must pass through these foundation stages to reach the rest of the house.

🔀 The Level 4 Crossroads

Level 4 features multiple exits: the Upper Exit warps to Level 5, the Middle Exit warps to Level 6, the Bottom Exit warps to Level 7, and the safe water tunnel leads to Secret Level 13!

⚠️ The Level 10 Decoy Trap

All campaign paths converge on Level 10. Beware: the prominent mouse hole on the upper right is a fake that sends you back to Level 1! Only the central electric portal unlocks Level 16.

🏆 Level 16: Grand Finale

The ultimate laboratory gauntlet with high-voltage spark timings, precision block sliding, and the final victory room.

02 The 4 Major Secrets & Illusions

Visual walkthroughs with human landmarks and clear routes
Secret 1 — Level 1

The Subterranean Cheese Vault

Hidden right inside the first stage is an underground bunker packed with five high-value cheese wedges worth 1,250+ bonus points!

  • 1
    Start & Climb: From your starting position on the bottom-left floor, hop up the staggered brick ledges along the far left wall until you reach the upper reservoir platform.
  • 2
    Cross the Safe Water: At the right end of this upper ledge sits a shallow blue water pool. Jump straight in! This pool is an illusion and will not drown you.
  • 3
    Drop Down the Secret Chute: Walk into the brick wall on the right side of the pool. The bricks are hollow! You will drop down a vertical hidden shaft directly into the sealed vault.
  • 4
    Collect & Escape: Grab the cheese wedges and coins, then walk onto the single false floor tile at the bottom-left of the vault to drop safely back onto the main floor.
Secret 2 — Level 3

The 118-Tile Solid Brick Fortress Maze

At the far bottom-right corner of Level 3 sits an imposing wall of red brick that seems completely impenetrable. In fact, 118 of these bricks are completely hollow!

  • 1
    Approach the Fortress: Travel across the floorboards to the far bottom-right corner of Level 3 until you are standing directly in front of the massive brick wall.
  • 2
    Step Inside the Wall: Walk directly into the face of the brick wall along the floor line. Your rat will slip seamlessly inside the brick structure.
  • 3
    Follow the S-Curve Blueprint: Ascend the hidden interior stairs, move rightward past the interior pillar, and drop down into the secluded bottom chamber.
  • 4
    Warp to Secret Level 12: At the end of the hidden corridor sits an authentic mouse hole that transports you into Secret Level 12 (Crystal Caves)!
Secret 3 — Level 4

Safe Illusionary Water Channel to Bonus Level 13

In the flooded basement of Level 4, an underground trench looks identical to lethal drowning water. The left half is actually shallow safe water!

  • 1
    Locate the Basement Water: Descend to the lower-left section of Level 4 where water floods the basement floor.
  • 2
    Wade Through the Safe Water: Step onto the water starting at the left side of the platform. The first 11 water tiles are completely harmless—your rat wades freely through the shallow channel.
  • 3
    Beware Deep Water: Do not travel past the marked boundary into the deep water pool to the right, which causes instant drowning!
  • 4
    Enter Secret Level 13: Wade all the way to the far left rock face and enter the mouse hole to access Secret Bonus Level 13!
Secret 4 — Level 10

Decoy Mouse Hole Trap vs. True Grand Finale Portal

Level 10 tests whether you've been paying attention to the game's deceptive tricks. A prominent mouse hole in the upper-right chamber is a deadly decoy!

  • 1
    Avoid the Upper-Right Decoy: The mouse hole labeled with a Pack Rat sign in the upper right is guarded by an armed mousetrap and sits on a 5-tile false floor that collapses into a lethal spike pit. Even if reached, it resets you back to Level 1!
  • 2
    Climb to the Machine Room: Instead, navigate into the middle-left generator chamber (shown in Panel B).
  • 3
    Dodge the Electric Spark: Time your jumps carefully to avoid the crawling green spark patrolling the circuit.
  • 4
    Activate the Grand Finale: Enter the mouse hole nestled between the high-voltage coils to trigger the warp to Level 16: The Grand Finale!

04 Essential Survival Mechanics

Core platforming physics and interaction formulas

🧵 Thread Spools (Roll & Climb)

Spools serve as mobile platforms and ladders. Walk into a spool while holding the Push key to roll it across flat ground. When touching a vertical column of spools, hold Up to climb upward, or Down to descend safely.

🔴 Trampoline Rubber Bands

Landing on red rubber bands bounces you upward automatically. Pro Tip: Hold down the Jump button as you strike the band to execute a Super Trampoline Bounce, flying nearly twice as high! You can also jump upward through rubber bands from below without bumping your head.

🧊 Ice Blocks (The 3-Push Rule)

Ice blocks are heavier than standard spools. Hold Push and press forward three distinct times to slide the block. If standing on top of an ice block, crouching down three times will dislodge it below you, dropping you into lower chambers.

⚡ Electric Current Timing

Electric wires carrying green sparks will electrocute the Pack Rat on contact. Memorize the spark's patrol cycle—it moves in a fixed loop between wire junctions. Time your jumps when the spark moves away from your landing zone.

🐀 Enemy Rat Direction Fix

Enemy rats patrol fixed floor ledges. Unlike older buggy revisions where rats walked backwards, authentic enemy rats always face the exact direction they are moving. Wait for them to turn away before making your run across the ledge!

☠️ Emergency Suicide Key (K)

If you accidentally slide an ice block against a wall and trap yourself, press the K key on your keyboard. This restarts the current stage instantly without resetting your high scores or kicking you back to the title screen.

🛡️ Testing God Mode (I)

Press I or click the GOD button on the arcade header to toggle Invincible & Unlimited Lives mode. In God Mode, you gain 99 lives and complete immunity to mousetraps, live electrical circuits, deep water, and enemy rats for effortless level testing!
